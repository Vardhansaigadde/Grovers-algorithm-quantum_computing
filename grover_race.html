What real quantum hardware did

The same search run on a real IBM quantum computer (ibm_kingston). The 2-qubit circuit is short and stays accurate. The 3-qubit circuit needs many more gates, and noise pulls the result down.

This page simulates Grover exactly with state-vector math. Names and usernames are synthetic, and passwords are drawn from a small list of common passwords. The oracle marks a known index, as in standard demonstrations. A real database would use a hash index, which beats unsorted linear search.
